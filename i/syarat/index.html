2. Tentang layanan2. About the Service

Smartphone Empire adalah game simulasi bisnis ponsel dari 1 Januari 2000 sampai era AI. Kamu merekrut tim, meriset teknologi, merancang ponsel dari berbagai komponen, menjual produk, mengatur bank dan promosi, berinvestasi di bursa, dan bersaing dengan perusahaan kompetitor.

Smartphone Empire is a phone-business simulation game that runs from January 1, 2000 to the AI era. You hire a team, research technology, design phones from many components, sell products, manage banks and promotions, invest on the stock exchange, and compete against rival companies.

The game is free to play in the browser and can be installed as a PWA. The blog covers smartphone news, specs, rumors, and release schedules. Features may be added, changed, or discontinued as the game is updated.
